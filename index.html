Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue finds safe, loving homes for animals in need. We work with local shelters, foster families, and volunteers to give every dog and cat a second chance at a happy life.

Our Impact

Here is what our community has helped us do this year:

  • 500+ animals placed in loving homes
  • 120 active volunteers
  • 85 foster families
  • 12 community partner organizations

How You Can Help

There are many ways to support our animals and our mission:

  • Adopt a pet and give them a permanent home
  • Foster an animal while they wait for adoption
  • Volunteer your time at events or at the shelter
  • Donate to support food, shelter, and medical care

Visit our Services page to learn more, or go to Contact to get in touch with our team.
