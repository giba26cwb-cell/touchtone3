Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue started in 2010, when a small group of neighbors began fostering stray animals out of their own homes. Word spread, and more volunteers joined. Today, we are a full nonprofit organization with a shelter, a foster network, and a team of dedicated staff and volunteers, but our goal has never changed: every animal deserves a safe, loving home.

Core Values

  • Compassion: Every animal is treated with kindness and patience.
  • Transparency: We are honest with adopters, donors, and our community.
  • Community: We believe rescue work is stronger when neighbors help each other.
