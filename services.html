Adoption Process

Adopting a pet from us is simple. First, browse our available animals and find one who fits your home. Next, fill out a short adoption application. Then, meet your future pet in person at our shelter. Finally, once your application is approved, you can bring your new family member home.

Foster Program

Fostering gives an animal a safe home while they wait to be adopted. Foster families provide food, a comfortable place to sleep, and daily care. We provide guidance, supplies, and cover all medical costs. Fostering can last anywhere from a few days to a few months.

Volunteer Roles

We have volunteer roles for every schedule and skill level. Click "Add to My Interests" on any role below to save it — we will remember your choices when you visit our Contact page.

  • Dog Walker: Walk and exercise animals at the shelter
  • Event Helper: Support adoption events and fundraisers
  • Administrative Support: Help with paperwork and phone calls
  • Transport Driver: Drive animals to vet visits or foster homes

Ready to get started? Visit our Contact page to request a volunteer or foster spot.
